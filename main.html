Artisan Noir

The garment
as art.

Before leather. Before Hamburg.
There was Paris — and the art of the cut.

She trained at
Guy Laroche, Paris.
She knows what a garment
can say — without a word.
The vision
"Black is not sad.
Black is poetry.
How do you imagine
a poet?"
Yohji Yamamoto

Artisan Noir is Giulia's most personal work. Not leather. Not silver. Pure garment — structured, dark, made to be worn like a second identity.

Trained in the tradition of Parisian Haute Couture at Guy Laroche, she understands construction — how a seam can sculpt, how a cut can speak louder than colour.

The aesthetic is close to Yamamoto, Ann Demeulemeester — the dark European avant-garde. Anti-fashion. Anti-trend. Each piece a statement of identity, not a statement of season.

An der Fortunagasse in Zürich befindet sich das Atelier eines Schatzsuchers. Schätze aus Leder und Silber, handgemacht. Jede Jacke ein Einzelstück. Die Besitzerin, Giulia Christen, passt perfekt zum Inventar: elegant, dunkel, die wilden schwarzen Locken.
